The League

ICL is a competitive and community-driven league where schools compete in high-intensity blitz tournaments. It's not a club — it's a circuit, run the way real team chess is run: by board order, by the clock, and by results that get posted the same night.

A1

Competitive

Fast time controls, real stakes, and a standings table that updates after every tournament — every school is playing for a position, not just a trophy on the shelf.

A2

Community

Players meet rivals from across the city and beyond, coaches trade notes, and a tournament night becomes something students actually look forward to.

A3

Expanding

Uzbekistan first, then the wider region — the goal is a Central Asian school chess circuit, not a one-city event.

How It Works

Every match is organized the way club and national team chess is organized: by board. Here's what a school signs up for.

B1

Field your team — three players, one lineup.

Each school enters a team of three players, seated Board 1 through Board 3 in order of strength. That order is set before the tournament and holds for the night.

B2

Show up and play — board by board.

Board 1 plays Board 1, Board 2 plays Board 2, Board 3 plays Board 3. Teams meet at the venue and play their assigned board order against the opposing school, blitz time controls, over the board — no substitutions mid-match.

B3

Standings go up — results the same night.

Board results, match scores, and tournament highlights are compiled and shared right after the final game finishes — so every school knows exactly where it stands.
